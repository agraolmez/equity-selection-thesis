# Bocconi Bachelor Final Paper formatting record

This submission copy follows Bocconi University's 2025-2026 Bachelor Final
Paper guidance, which applies to the October 2026 graduation session, and the
University's *Guide to Writing the Final Paper*.

## Applied submission rules

- A4 portrait pages (21 x 29.7 cm).
- Left and right margins of 2.5 cm; matching 2.5 cm top and bottom margins.
- Arial, 12 pt body text, with exact 24 pt line spacing (approximately 29
lines on a full prose page).
- The DOCX specifies Arial throughout. The final submission PDF was exported
  through Microsoft Word and embeds Arial; the local LibreOffice QA render is
  retained only as a renderer-specific comparison.
- Four opening pages with no printed content or page number.
- Contents, introduction, body, conclusion, and references begin after those
  four pages; visible numbering restarts at 1.
- No author name, student number, Bocconi seal, title page, or abstract appears
  in the submitted paper body.
- The abstract is supplied separately as plain text and remains below the
  4,000-character limit.
- The paper includes a concise declaration of AI-tool use and its purposes.
  Bocconi's current Student Code of Conduct requires students submitting a
  thesis or other evaluated written work to declare whether AI tools were used
  and, if so, specify their purpose. The wording should be reviewed with Tutor
  Professor Andrea Beltratti before submission.
- The final PDF is A4, below 10 MB, and the submission copy is named
  `LF3245801.pdf`.

## Length and visual-evidence decision

The submitted body is 33 numbered pages, including contents and references,
plus the four required blank opening pages. This is consistent with Bocconi's
guidance of approximately 30 pages. Eight compact, source-backed figures are
retained because they clarify the feature set, chronology, selector evidence,
rank-to-weight conversion, validation choice, benchmark comparison, and the
return and exposure trade-offs of the 2.05-times specification. Technical
appendices remain outside the submission copy.
